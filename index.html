Method

How the model scores teams

Population

Log-scaled, so a larger country gets talent-pool credit without making Brazil or the United States automatically unbeatable.

Climate

Rewards countries near a mild annual temperature and penalizes extreme heat or cold that can limit year-round outdoor play.

Wealth

Log-scaled GDP per person at purchasing-power parity, used as a proxy for schools, academies, training grounds, and coaching capacity.

FIFA ranking

Converts rank into a strength score, giving the current generation of players the largest default influence.
